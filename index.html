🛡️ Green & Safe Diwali Guide

Celebrate With Safety, Joy & Mindfulness

At MOTION VAULT STUDIO, we believe true celebration spreads light without casting shadows. Here are essential safety recommendations for you, your family, and community:

🐾

Cherish Our Animal Friends

Animals have ultra-sensitive hearing. Keep pets indoors in a cozy, secure room with closed windows. Play soothing music, provide fresh water, and never tease or burst crackers near street animals.

Pet Friendly
🌿

Choose Green & Earth-Safe

If bursting crackers, choose certified SWAS, STAR, SAFAL green crackers with 30-40% lower emissions and free of toxic barium. Better yet, light up with organic earthen lamps!

Eco-Conscious
🧯

Cotton Attire & First-Aid Ready

Wear fitted cotton clothing; avoid synthetic fabrics, dupattas, or loose garments near flames. Always keep a bucket of water, sand, and a standard burn-relief kit (like silver sulfadiazine) within arm's reach.

Fire Preparedness
👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Active Parental Supervision

Never let young children light crackers unaccompanied. Maintain at least an arm's length when lighting sparklers, and never attempt to relight an unexploded cracker or inspect it closely.

Family Care
🏥

Respect Hospitals & Elders

Observe strict silent hours around hospitals, elderly shelters, and residential care facilities. Avoid loud, high-decibel explosives. Celebrate with luminous lights and sweet treats instead!

Community Harmony
🧹

Responsible Post-Celebration Cleanup

Dispose of burnt sparklers and cracker casings by quenching them in a water bucket before bagging them. Keep streets clean and prevent accidental fires or burns to sanitation workers.

Clean Neighborhood
