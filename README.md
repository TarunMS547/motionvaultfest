## 🌟 Highlights & Key Features

### 1. ✍️ Individual & Personalized User Experience
- **Dynamic Name & Vibe Personalization**: Visitors can enter their name and choose their celebration blessing vibe (*Wealth & Prosperity*, *Success & Ambition*, *Peace & Health*, *Creative Energy & Joy*).
- **URL Parameter Support**: Shareable personalized links (e.g. `index.html?name=Karthik&vibe=success`).
- **One-Click Social Sharing**: Instant WhatsApp sharing pre-formatted with personalized greetings and direct clipboard copying.
- **Downloadable High-Res Poster**: In-browser HTML5 Canvas generates an 800×1000px celebratory greeting card featuring the user's name, personalized blessing, glowing diya illustration, and sign-offs from **Tarun M S**, **Sow Karthik S**, and **Vishnu Karthikeyaa J**.

### 2. 🪔 Interactive Sacred Diya Chamber
- **Pancha Diyas**: 5 clay lamps symbolizing Prosperity, Wisdom, Health, Peace, and Joy.
- **Realistic Flame Simulation**: Click or tap to light individual diyas, triggering harmonic temple bell chimes and warm flickering light auras.
- **Grand Celestial Blessing**: Lighting all 5 diyas unlocks the grand blessing and automatically triggers a multi-burst celebration firework show.

### 3. 🎆 Heavy GUI Firecracker Sandbox & Physics Engine
- **Pollution-Free Virtual Crackers**:
  - **Golden Sparkler (Phooljhadi)**: Click & drag anywhere across the screen to draw living sparks.
  - **Fountain Anar**: 15-foot sparkling golden and violet star fountain.
  - **Zipping Chakri**: Ground spinner with radiant neon particle spirals.
  - **Sky Rockets**: High-altitude rockets that explode into multi-tiered rings and chrysanthemums.
  - **Akashdeep Sky Lanterns**: Floating lanterns carrying glowing wishes gently into the night sky.
  - **Rangoli Confetti**: Colorful floral petal & gold glitter bursts.
- **Interactive Screen Clicks**: Tap or click anywhere on the background to launch fireworks.

### 4. 🛡️ Comprehensive Safe Celebrations Guide
- **Cherish Our Animal Friends**: Pet safety guidelines, sound reduction, calming indoor spaces.
- **Choose Green & Earth-Safe**: SWAS/STAR/SAFAL green cracker awareness & clay diya traditions.
- **Cotton Attire & First-Aid Ready**: Fire safety protocols and emergency readiness.
- **Active Parental Supervision**: Protecting children during festivities.
- **Respect Hospitals & Elders**: Silent zones and community harmony.
- **Responsible Cleanup**: Safe disposal methods for spent fireworks.
- **Interactive Safe Celebration Pledge**: Users can take the digital pledge and generate a certified safe celebration badge with their name.

### 5. 🎵 Zero-Dependency Web Audio API Sound Engine
- Built-in audio synthesis for:
  - Realistic firework explosions and bass thumps
  - Sizzling sparkler crackles
  - Rocket whistling ascents
  - Whirring chakri buzz
  - Resonant sacred temple bells
  - Ambient festive Indian classical melody (Raag Yaman scale)
- Sound & Music toggle controls in floating HUD.

## 🚀 How to Run

Simply open `index.html` in any modern web browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge). No build step, node modules, or server setup required!

Alternatively, you can run a local server:
```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
```
Then visit `http://localhost:8000` in your browser.
